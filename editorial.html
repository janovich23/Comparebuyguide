Our editorial approach

Useful buying advice starts with a clear explanation of the product's purpose and its tradeoffs. These are the standards for content on Compare Buy Guide.

Buying guides and product reviews

A feature-based buying guide explains what to look for. A research-based comparison should identify its sources and the models compared. Hands-on testing means a product was actually used; we will describe that testing when it occurs. Our current jump-starter guide is a feature-based guide, not a tested product ranking.

Specifications and compatibility

Product details can vary by model and change over time. Readers should confirm compatibility, included accessories, warranty terms, and instructions with the manufacturer before purchasing. A general guide cannot establish whether a specific model suits every situation.

Prices and availability

We do not currently publish live product prices. If prices are included in future guides, their source and timing should be clear; the retailer's current checkout price determines what you pay.

Affiliate relationships

Any active affiliate relationship should be disclosed near the relevant shopping links as well as on our disclosure page. Paid placement should be clearly labeled. We do not present commissions as proof of product quality.

Corrections

To flag an error, email us with the page and the specific statement. We can then check the detail and update the page where appropriate.
