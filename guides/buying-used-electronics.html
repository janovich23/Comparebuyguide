Used and refurbished electronics: what to check before buying

Compare the actual listing, not just the product model. Two listings for the same laptop or camera may differ substantially in condition, accessories and warranty.

Six checks before checkout

  1. Condition: Read the seller's full description and look for photos of the actual unit, including any damage.
  2. Seller: Review recent feedback, transaction history and the specific seller's return terms.
  3. Compatibility: Verify model number, region, ports, storage, accessories and any carrier restrictions.
  4. Battery and wear: Ask about battery health, shutter count or other wear indicators when relevant.
  5. Warranty and returns: Confirm who provides coverage, what is excluded and who pays return shipping.
  6. Total price: Include shipping, taxes, missing accessories and potential repair costs.

Refurbished versus used

“Refurbished” does not always mean manufacturer-refurbished. Check who inspected the item and what testing or warranty is included. Condition labels can vary by seller and marketplace program.
