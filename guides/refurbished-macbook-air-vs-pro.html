What matters more than the name?

Performance: The M4 Pro is designed for more demanding workloads; the standard M4 is capable for many everyday tasks. Check compatibility with your actual software.

Screen and portability: These particular listings offer a larger 15-inch Air display versus a 14-inch Pro. Consider size, weight, and display features, not just diagonal inches.

Condition: The Air listing was labeled Excellent – Refurbished, while the Pro was Good – Refurbished. Seller grading systems can vary. Read the actual condition description.

Battery and warranty: Ask about battery health, returns, and whether warranty coverage is provided. See our battery-health checklist.

For routine work and a larger display, start by comparing the Air. For heavier professional workloads, the Pro may justify paying more. The difference between the two asking prices above was $282 when checked, before any changes in shipping or taxes; that is not a current price quote.
